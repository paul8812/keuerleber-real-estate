About me

I'm Tim. I shoot weddings, cars and the reels that come out of both. I work alone, from the first call to the final cut, so the person you talk to is the one holding the camera. The best frames happen the second you forget I'm there.

How it runs

First you write me.

Date, place, what you have in mind. I reply within 24 hours.

Then we talk, briefly.

About twenty minutes. After that you get a fixed quote in writing.

Then I shoot your day.

For as long as it runs. You won't notice me much.

You get photo, film, reels.

A sneak peek within a week, the full gallery and film a few weeks later.

Questions

How far ahead should we book?

For weddings, six to twelve months, because summer Saturdays go first. Car shoots and reels usually work within a few weeks.

Do you travel?

Yes. I'm based in Hof and shoot across Germany and abroad. Travel is part of the quote and agreed upfront.

When do we get the pictures?

A sneak peek within a week. The full gallery and the film follow within a few weeks, depending on the season.

Photo, film or both?

Either, or both from the same person. Both is the fun part: photos and film tell the same day.

What does it cost?

After a short call I send a fixed quote for exactly what you need. No surprises.

Do you edit every photo?

Every photo you get is picked and colour-graded by me. Heavier retouching on request.
