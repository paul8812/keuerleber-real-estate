About me

I've been shooting photo and film for about four years: weddings, cars, and the reels that come out of both.

What I care about is that it never feels like a shoot. The best frames happen the second you forget I'm there.

I work alone, from the first call to the final edit, so the person you talk to is the one holding the camera and the one cutting your film.

I'm based in Hof and travel for most jobs, across Germany and further when it fits.

Questions, answered

How far ahead should we book? For weddings, six to twelve months is ideal, because summer Saturdays go first. Car shoots and reels usually work within a few weeks.

Do you travel? Yes. I'm based in Hof and shoot across Germany and abroad. Travel is part of the quote and agreed upfront.

When do we get the pictures? A sneak peek within a week. The full gallery and the film follow within a few weeks, depending on the season.

Photo, film, or both? Either, or both from the same person. Both is where it gets interesting: the photos and the film tell the same day.

What does it cost? After a short call I send a fixed quote for exactly what you need, so there are no surprises later.

Do you handle social media too? Yes. I plan, film and cut reels and can look after a channel month by month.

What if the weather turns? We plan a second option for every outdoor part. Car shoots move to the next good day.

Do you edit every photo? Every photo you get is selected and colour-graded by me. Heavier retouching on request.
